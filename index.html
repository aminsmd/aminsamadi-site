Building agentic AI systems. Studying how people and AI work together.

As an engineer

I build agentic AI systems end to end: multi-agent orchestration, context engineering, tool calling and MCP integrations, GraphRAG with long-term agent memory, evals and observability, and post-training experiments with SFT and GRPO, shipped as FastAPI, Django, and Next.js services on AWS.

At ROIAI One I built a root-cause analysis agent for B2B financial claims that led to a paid pilot. At UC Irvine I designed and led TRAIL, a platform that embeds LLM teammates in real-time team chat, load-tested for 50+ concurrent participants.

As a researcher

I hold a Ph.D. in Education (HCI and Learning Analytics) from UC Irvine, completed in October 2026 under Nia Nixon. My dissertation asks what happens inside a team when one member is an AI: who leads, who gets heard, and how a teammate's personality and memory change what the group does.

The work has appeared at LAK, EDM, and AIED, won the Best Short Paper Award at EDM 2025, and received the UCI School of Education's Vandell Dissertation Research Award.

January to September 2026

AI Scientist Intern, ROIAI One

ROIAI One builds agentic AI for B2B financial claims: disputing and preventing invalid retailer chargebacks. I built the original dispute-automation agent that replaced a manual analyst workflow, then the root-cause analysis workflow, a multi-agent system with GraphRAG and long-term memory that works out who is at fault for a claim and how to prevent it. The founder demoed it to customers and it led to a paid pilot. I also built the tracing and eval tooling, portal automation across five retailer portals, and a prototype pipeline that distills agent traces into a small model.

May 2023 to present

TRAIL: Team Research and AI Integration Lab

TRAIL embeds configurable LLM teammates in real-time, multi-party chatrooms so that human–AI collaboration can be studied under controlled conditions. I designed the platform and lead its engineering: a four-stage agent pipeline with two-tier memory, a simulation and replay tool, and experiment infrastructure for randomized, multi-session studies. It has run a six-session classroom study and a pilot RCT comparing human-only and human–AI teams.
