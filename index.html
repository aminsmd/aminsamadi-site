Studying how people and AI work together. Building the systems to find out.

As a researcher

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Education at UC Irvine, working on human–computer interaction and learning analytics with Nia Nixon. My dissertation asks what happens inside a team when one member is an AI: who leads, who gets heard, and how a teammate's personality and timing change what the group decides.

The work has appeared at LAK, EDM, and AIED, won the Best Short Paper Award at EDM 2025, and received the UCI School of Education's Vandell Dissertation Research Award.

As an engineer

I'm an AI Scientist Intern at ROI-AI One in San Francisco, where I take LLM agent systems from idea to production: multi-agent workflows, evaluation and observability tooling, document and portal automation, and the pipeline that turns agent traces into training data.

Before that I designed and built TRAIL, the platform my lab's studies run on, and I keep it running on AWS for sessions with 50+ concurrent participants.

May 2023 to present

TRAIL: Team Research and AI Integration Lab

TRAIL puts configurable LLM teammates into real-time, multi-party chatrooms so that human–AI collaboration can be studied under controlled conditions. I designed the platform, architected the AI teammate as a multi-agent pipeline with hierarchical memory and a Big Five persona compiler, and built the survey, randomization, and data-capture tooling the studies depend on. I also run the studies: coding schemes, instruments, and the statistical analysis.
