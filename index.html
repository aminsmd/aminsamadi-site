I build agentic AI systems, from multi-agent orchestration and agent memory to evals and post-training. I completed my Ph.D. in Education (HCI / Learning Analytics) at UC Irvine in October 2026, advised by Dr. Nia Nixon. My dissertation research on human–AI collaboration in teams received the UCI School of Education's Vandell Dissertation Research Award.

I designed and lead TRAIL (Team Research and AI Integration Lab), a platform that puts configurable LLM teammates into real-time team chat to study how personality, memory, and communication shape human–AI collaboration.

Research Focus

AI Teammates in Human Teams

How an AI teammate changes who speaks, who leads, and what a team decides, and how to design agents that know when to speak and when to stay quiet.

Agent Memory and Persona

How memory and persona design shape agent behavior over long interactions, including how memory amplifies personality traits and how to keep agents from drifting.

Evaluating Agentic Systems

Measuring whether agents behave as intended, from persona-fidelity tests and human-rated benchmarks to tracing and evals for production agents.
